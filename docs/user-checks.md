# Checks for the user

Things only the user can settle, by playing the original in the emulator, by listening, or in full-game testing. Each says what to do and what to look for, and links to where the port's reading is written up. When one is settled, the finding goes in that doc and the item comes off this list. A check far into the story needn't be played to: a save can be written into the pak beside the ROM, at the place with the flags and party it needs (`aidyn-renewed save to-pak`, [saves](systems/saves.md#the-originals-save)).

## Setting up a check

The user needn't play to a check: a save is written into the Controller Pak beside 1.0's ROM, at the place, flags and party the check needs, and the user loads it from the title in `tools/ares.ps1 v10`. Every save so far has loaded. The format and the commands are in [saves](systems/saves.md#making-and-checking-saves).

1. **Start from a save of the port's.** `aidyn-renewed save from-pak <rom> <slot> --pak "roms/<1.0 rom basename> - story saves.pak" --out <save>` takes one of the four story saves (A before the final encounter, B before Shamsuk's Tower, C before Rooughah, D in Terminor, each with the [checkpoint](reference-rig.md#checkpoint-library) captured at the same place); `aidyn-renewed save from <checkpoint>` or `aidyn-renewed save new` do too. Pick the one nearest the check in the story, and say which flags were changed to get there.
2. **Find what the check needs.** The map object that starts the script or holds the place: search the installed base plugin's `records/maps/*.json` for the script's ID (its stage, position, radius and enabling flag), and `records/mapsets.json` for the chunk that places the map. `aidyn-renewed flag <n>` says what sets, clears and reads a flag, and what a derived one is made of. `aidyn-renewed dialogue <rom> <number>` dumps the script, for the path to the node that matters.
3. **Edit it** with the scripts in `tools/saves/`, each run as `dotnet run tools/saves/<script>.cs -- …`:
   - `party.cs <in> <out> --remove <entity hex,…> --add <entity hex,…>` changes the party by the scripts' own leave and join, so the party's flags follow.
   - `edit.cs <in> <out> --at <map set> <chunk> <x> <z> [<y>] --face <x> <z> --set <n,…> --clear <n,…> --give <item ID> <n> --label <text>` stands the player on the floor under a point of the chunk (swept down on the port's collision; give `y` indoors, or it lands on the roof), facing another point, sets or clears plain flags, and adds items to the inventory.
   - `flags.cs <save> <n,…>` prints flags, derived ones included, as the save loads: check the object's enabling flag holds, and that what should be hidden is.
4. **Write it**: `aidyn-renewed save to-pak <rom> <save> A`. Close ares first, since it writes its pak back as it closes. Slot A is the checks'; the command keeps the pak as it was first (`<pak>.<time>.bak`), and the story saves stay untouched in `- story saves.pak`, which the tests read.
5. **For the runtime**, the same JSON loads with `tools/godot.ps1 --path src/AidynRenewed.Game -- --save <path>`. Keep it in the gitignored `out/` and give the user the path: what Claude writes under `%LOCALAPPDATA%` lands in the Claude app's private copy, not the data folder the user's runtime reads. The saves made for the checks so far are in `out/user-checks/`.
6. **Ask exactly what the user saw**, step by step, and whose animation it was: in the lighthouse keeper's check, "they play their animations" meant their ordinary talk, not the line's own animation, and a fix built on the wrong reading had to be undone. Then the finding goes in the system's doc, the item comes off this list, and the port's fixes follow [ADR 0018](adr/0018-the-port-fixes-bugs-and-sets-enhancements.md) and, for the game's data, [ADR 0029](adr/0029-content-fixes-wait-for-a-bugfix-mod.md).

## Scripts and characters

- **Sholeh in Ugarit after she joins** (`aidyn:dialogue/0x04AD`), in full-game testing: is she there again once she's joined, and what keeps her away when the party has no free place? The data doesn't show it. [wanderers](systems/wanderers.md#joining)
- **Too many wanderers:** does the original crash or leave some parties out in the 3×3 around Overworld H13, with a new game's flags? It has room for 39 at once, and 44 parties there would get one. [wanderers](systems/wanderers.md)
- **Item spells' charges:** cast a spell from a sword, armor or a ring (not a wand or scroll) several times in one battle and across battles. Does it ever run out, and does it come back with time? The code spends charges only from wands and scrolls worn as gear, and never recharges any. [magic](systems/magic.md#gear)
- **The Ring of Healing in trek:** wear it on a hurt member and walk about by day. Do their hit points climb about one a frame, many a second, or slowly or not at all? The code heals one each time trek ages the effects, once a drawn frame, and the port does it once a 30 fps frame. [magic](systems/magic.md#not-settled)

## Movement

- **Wedged against a leaning rock face:** in the original, walk Alaron hard into the steep plain rock at Overworld A22, feet about (46.7, −26.3, 40.1) (the face just south of there), and then at D14 (55.2, −16.1, 19.1) and Q25 (88.3, −2.1, 46.5). Turn about and walk off each time. The model of 1.0's frames says he walks off at A22, where the port wedged him, but can stick at D14 and Q25, unable to steer or move until something else moves him. Does he? The port lets him steer after a quarter of a second held so. Then, in the port, look for the crevices between rock faces at B15 (76.5, −26.9, 30.0) and H08 (71.4, −19.6, 55.2): can play get him down into one, where no way out works? [movement](systems/movement.md#wedged-against-a-leaning-plain-wall)

## Menus

- **A new game from the load menu** (the runtime, with a save from `aidyn-renewed save new` or the editor's new game): load it from the System tab's Load. Does it play the opening cinematic, then the opening's script on its stage, then trek in Overworld I08, as a plain launch does? [saves](systems/saves.md#the-format)

## Combat

- **A failed or resisted spell** (any battle with a caster; a low-level spell fails often): when "fails to cast the spell" shows, does a puff of coloured smoke rise at the caster's feet, orange, yellow and pale blue fading to violet, over about a second and a half, with no white glow or darkened sky? When "resists the spell" shows, does a green and yellow one fall at the target? Both are read from the code (actors `0x1CAA` and `0x37F1`); the port draws both. [combat](systems/combat.md#what-spells-look-like)
- **Darkness or Light in a battle**, and **Wall of Bones, Frozen Doom or Web of Starlight** (any save with a member who knows them): does the sky swing to night or noon over a second and back as the effect ends, and does a held enemy stop its idle animation? Both are read from the code. [combat](systems/combat.md#what-spells-look-like)
- **A camp ambush on terrain 27** (the Overworld's N25, O24–O26, P24 and P25, east of the desert, with two of Abrecan, Godric and Rheda joined so that 5004 holds): camp there until it's ambushed. The original reads no enemy for that terrain, so the battle has none. Does a battle start and end at once as a win (with the post-battle window), does it never start, or does the game crash? Until it's settled, the port says the party is ambushed and trek comes back with no battle. [camping](systems/camping.md#the-ambushs-enemies)
- **The post-battle window's member:** after a battle in which you paused on someone other than Alaron's turn (Start on Brenna's turn, then back), does the post-battle window open on the member whose turn you paused on, rather than Alaron? The code's every write to the member says so, and the port does the same. [ui](systems/ui.md#after-a-battle)
- **The Shadow's battle** (a save before the merge, flag 566 clear, at either meeting): with the Horn, does the Shadow walk straight at Alaron and, reaching him, cut to Shadow Self Merge, with no post-battle window, and trek back where the battle began? Without it (drop the Horn first), does each round's end hurt whoever is near it, (10 − distance)² each, for four rounds, and then "The Shadow dissipates" hold about a second before the fade? Is anyone within 10 of it held to a field of 1, with "caught in the vortex of the Shadow"? A checkpoint inside either battle would let the port be replayed against it. [combat](systems/combat.md#the-special-battles)
- **The goblins' darts** (the Goblin Ambush, a new game): does the dart leave the goblin's hand about two fifths into its throw, and does Alaron react as it lands? [combat](systems/combat.md#an-attack)

## Sound

- **Wrapped pitches:** music 0x05DD and 0x05DE, and sounds such as 0x0759 and 0x075F, have notes above 65,535 Hz that the original keeps in 16 bits, so they'd play far too low. The port does the same. Listen for a low growl or rumble against `aidyn-renewed audio`'s render. [audio](formats/audio.md#the-event-stream)
- **The sound volume at half:** by the code, the menu's sounds take the sound volume twice and the containers' three times. At half sound volume, are they a quarter as loud? The port plays them so. [audio](systems/audio.md#volumes)
- **The post-battle windows in the runtime**, after a battle won in the forest that gains Alaron a level (as in the prologue): are the training and loot windows silent but for the menu's own sounds, with no ambient sound or rain under them, and does the level-up jingle (0x05E8) play about a second after the scroll starts unrolling, as in the original? [ui](systems/ui.md#after-a-battle)
